## Your Immediate Action Plan: What to Do RIGHT NOW

This extension is a gift, but it's one you must seize without delay. Here’s exactly what you need to do today:

### Step 1: Breathe, Then Gather Your Information

Take a moment to calm your nerves. Then, immediately begin compiling all necessary documents. This typically includes:
*   Your child's academic records (transcripts, test scores).
*   Proof of residency.
*   Income verification (tax returns, pay stubs).
*   Any other specific documents requested by the TEFA program.
*   A list of schools or programs your child is applying to or attending.

### Step 2: Access the TEFA Application Portal

Don't wait. Log back into the official TEFA application portal. If you started an application, it should still be there. If not, begin a new one. Familiarize yourself with the updated interface and any new instructions.

### Step 3: Start Your Application (Even If Incomplete!)

The most crucial step is to **begin the application process today.** Even if you don't have every single document perfectly in order, start filling out what you can. This creates a timestamp, shows your intent, and allows you to save your progress. Don't let the pursuit of perfection prevent you from starting.

## Missing Documents? Here's How to Fast-Track Your Application

One of the biggest hurdles for families was missing documentation. The extension gives you time, but you can still fast-track your submission.

### Provisional Submission Strategy

Many programs allow for a provisional submission. If you're missing a specific document that will take time to acquire (e.g., a new transcript, a specific tax form), complete the rest of your application and make a clear note in the relevant section about the missing item and when you expect to provide it. Some systems allow you to upload a placeholder document explaining the delay.

### Contacting Support Immediately

Don't guess. If you're unsure about a missing document, contact TEFA support services *today*. Ask for specific guidance on how to proceed with an incomplete application or what alternatives are accepted. Get their advice in writing if possible.

### Prioritize Key Information

Focus on accurately completing the most critical sections first:
*   Applicant and family demographic information.
*   Financial data that you *do* have readily available.
*   School choices.

This ensures the core of your application is solid while you chase down the remaining pieces.
